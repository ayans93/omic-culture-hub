About the Talk

As part of the exhibition "Afterimage," OMIC Cultural Hub presents Emirati Visions: A Conversation on Video Art — a screening and panel discussion exploring video art in the UAE. Panelists Dr. Nujoom Alghanem and Khalil Abdulwahid join moderator Valentina della Rocca to discuss their work, followed by an open Q&A and a closing screening of a three-work playlist.

Exhibition Artists

Works screened as part of "Afterimage."

  • Nujoom Al Ghanem

    Emirati visual artist, poet, and multi-award-winning film director with eleven poetry collections and over twenty films. Represented the UAE at the Venice Biennale in 2017 and 2019.

    @nujoomalghanem

  • Khalil Abdulwahid

    Contemporary Emirati visual artist spanning painting, video art, and mixed media, trained under the late Hassan Sharif. Work shown in Abu Dhabi, Berlin, and the United States.

    @khalulalwahid

  • Ali Ettehad

    Director, writer, and visual/performing artist. His RAZVARZI series (2007–present) spans video art, installation, performance, and film, rooted in Middle Eastern mysticism and Persian philosophy.

    @aliettehad

  • Mohammad Ali Famori

    Indian-born Iranian visual artist and curator specializing in video art, glitch aesthetics, and sacred geometry. Curated Iran's first glitch video art group exhibition.

    @designfam

  • Beatriz Minguez de Molina

    Visual artist and architect exploring the relationship between people and nature through image and video. Shown at festivals including FIVA, Bideodromo, and the Granada Film Archive.

    @bea_mdm
