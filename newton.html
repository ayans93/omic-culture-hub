About the Film

Drishyam Films presents Newton, directed by Amit V. Masurkar and produced by Manish Mundra. A rookie government clerk, Nutan “Newton” Kumar (Rajkummar Rao), is assigned to oversee free and fair elections in a Naxal-controlled region of Chhattisgarh. Despite the apathy of the security forces escorting him and the looming threat of guerrilla attacks, he refuses to compromise on conducting a fair vote — a quiet, absurdist standoff between one man's idealism and a system that has long stopped expecting it.

Newton premiered in the Forum section of the 67th Berlin International Film Festival, where it won the CICAE Art Cinema Award, and went on to win the National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Hindi. It was India's official submission for Best Foreign Language Film at the 90th Academy Awards.
