About the Exhibition

OMIC Cultural Hub officially opens with its inaugural exhibition, "Nothing Unusual," curated by Narges Soleimanzadeh at Goshi Warehouse City. The opening evening includes a panel discussion exploring the dialogue between contemporary art and videography — how the moving image is reshaping the way artists document, distort, and reimagine the everyday. The conversation brings together artists and filmmakers featured in the exhibition to discuss process, medium, and the blurred lines between fine art and film.
