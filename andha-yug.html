About the Play

One of the masterpieces of modern Hindi literature and theatre, Dharamvir Bharati's Andha Yug is a powerful geeti-natya (verse drama) set on the final day of the Mahabharata war.

Through evocative poetry and dramatic dialogue, it explores the devastating consequences of war, hatred, revenge and the loss of moral conscience. Though rooted in the Mahabharata, this timeless work speaks profoundly to our world today.

Join theatrewallas – a theatre group from UAE – at OMIC for a compelling evening of Hindi theatre, powerful words and timeless questions. Experience this extraordinary literary masterpiece brought to life through the power of the spoken word.
