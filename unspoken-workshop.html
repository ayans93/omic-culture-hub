WORKSHOP ACRYLIC PAINTING PORTRAITURE

UNSPOKEN: Reimagining the Portrait

Date

Saturday, October 24, 2026

Time

11:00 AM–1:00 PM

Venue

OMIC Cultural Hub

Price

AED 285 per person

Capacity

Max 15 participants

Level

Beginner to intermediate

Perks

Materials, a croissant and coffee included

Workshop Conductor

Sami Alkour

About the Workshop

A contemporary acrylic workshop led by artist Sami Alkour, exploring portraiture as mood and presence rather than physical likeness alone. Over two hours, work with expressive brushwork and layered acrylic technique to complete one small acrylic portrait on canvas to take home. No experience needed, and all materials are included, along with a croissant and coffee for every attendee.

Key Messages

  • Portraiture beyond physical likeness — mood and presence, not just a face.
  • Expressive brushwork and layered acrylic techniques.
  • Accessible to everyone, no painting experience required.
  • Leave with your own finished acrylic portrait on canvas.
  • Everything included — materials, a croissant and coffee.
  • Limited to 15 places.

About the Artist

  • Sami Alkour

    A visual artist and art educator with 12+ years of experience in academic drawing and painting. He holds an MFA in Painting & Drawing from Damascus University and has taught and exhibited across the Middle East. His teaching combines strong drawing foundations with expressive, contemporary painting methods.
