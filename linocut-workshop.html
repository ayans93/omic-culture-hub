About the Workshop

A hands-on relief printmaking workshop led by artist Elizaveta Pugacheva and an assistant. In just two hours, go from a blank sketch to several hand-printed copies of your own design — carving your own linoleum block and pulling your first prints. No experience needed, and all materials are included, along with a croissant and coffee for every attendee.

Key Messages

  • Learn linocut printing in two hours, from sketch to finished prints.
  • Hands-on: carve your own block and print multiple copies.
  • Choose a prepared design or bring your own.
  • Guidance from the artist and an assistant throughout.
  • Everything included — no experience needed.
  • Limited to 15 places.

What You'll Do

  • Meet the technique through a short intro and live demo.
  • Transfer a sketch onto a linoleum block.
  • Carve the design with safe carving tools.
  • Ink the block and print several copies.

About the Artist

  • Elizaveta Pugacheva

    An interdisciplinary artist working across painting, graphics, and textiles, exploring themes of emigration, cultural identity, and collective memory. Her practice draws on the aesthetics of computer graphics and internet culture — memes among them — often as a lens for critiquing online labor, while tracing her own biographical transformation of identity.

    @lizplease.art
